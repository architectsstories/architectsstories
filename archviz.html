ARCHVIZ · ARCHITECTURAL VISUALIZATION PROGRAMME

From Model to Visual Story.

A practical architectural visualization programme for students, architects and designers who want to move beyond basic rendering and learn how professional visual stories are created.

Knowing software ≠ knowing visualization.

Most architecture students learn the buttons — how to model, apply a material, hit render. But a convincing image isn't a checklist of tools. It's composition, light, restraint and story.

ARCHVIZ exists for the gap between "I know the software" and "I can make someone feel a space before it's built." We teach the thinking behind the render, not just the render itself.

Curriculum Overview

What You'll Learn

Nine building blocks that take a project from raw 3D model to a finished visual story worth publishing.

01

Visualization Fundamentals

How rendering engines see light, geometry and materials — and why that matters for every decision after.

02

Materials

Building believable surfaces — roughness, reflectivity, texture scale and real-world reference.

03

Lighting

Natural and artificial light setups that shape mood, depth and time of day.

04

Camera & Composition

Framing, focal length and the rules (and exceptions) behind a shot that holds attention.

05

Environment

Context, landscaping, entourage and atmosphere that ground a building in its site.

06

Post-Production

Color grading, compositing and retouching to take a render from good to publish-ready.

07

Animation

Camera paths, pacing and sequencing for architectural walkthroughs and flythroughs.

08

AI Workflows

Where AI tools genuinely speed up visualization work — and where they still can't replace judgment.

09

Visual Storytelling

Sequencing images and boards so a project reads as a narrative, not a folder of renders.

Programme Structure

Module by Module

Detailed Curriculum

Expand each level to see exactly what's covered, week by week.

Level 01

Foundation

Introduction to the Visualization Pipeline ＋
Understanding how models move from SketchUp or Revit into a render engine, scene setup basics, and the render pipeline end to end.
  • Importing and cleaning geometry
  • Scene organization and units
  • Your first test render
Basic Materials & Lighting ＋
Core material properties and simple lighting setups that already make a scene feel real.
  • PBR material basics
  • Sun and sky systems
  • Interior artificial lighting
Camera & Composition Basics ＋
Framing a shot with intent — eye level, focal length and the rule of thirds applied to buildings.
  • Choosing a hero angle
  • Depth of field basics
  • Common framing mistakes
First Composed Still Render ＋
Bringing fundamentals together into one fully composed exterior still, reviewed live with the cohort.
Level 02

Professional

Advanced Materials & Texturing ＋
Layered and procedural materials, texture scaling, and matching materials to real product references.
Environment & Atmosphere ＋
Landscaping, entourage, fog, and time-of-day lighting to place a building convincingly in its context.
Interior Visualization ＋
Lighting and staging interior spaces — balancing natural light, artificial fixtures and furnishing for mood.
Exterior Visualization ＋
Full building exteriors at hero-shot quality, including site context and multiple time-of-day variations.
Studio-Grade Post-Production ＋
Color grading, compositing render passes, and retouching in Photoshop to finish a render for publication.
Level 03

Advanced

Animation & Walkthroughs ＋
Planning and producing a camera walkthrough — pacing, transitions and rendering for video output.
AI-Enhanced Workflows ＋
Practical use of AI tools for upscaling, style exploration and speeding up iteration without losing control.
Visual Storytelling & Presentation Boards ＋
Sequencing stills, sections and details into a presentation board that reads as a narrative.
Final Project & Mentor Critique ＋
A complete visualization package for one project, developed over two weeks with structured mentor feedback.

Questions

FAQ

Do I need prior rendering experience? ＋
No. Foundation level starts from the basics of the visualization pipeline. If you already have experience, you can place directly into Professional or Advanced after a short skills check.
What software do I need to have installed? ＋
You'll need SketchUp or Revit for modeling and D5 Render for visualization. Photoshop is used for post-production. Setup guidance is provided before the programme starts.
How much time does this require each week? ＋
Expect one live session plus 4–6 hours of assignment work per week, more during the final project weeks.
Is this fully live or self-paced? ＋
Sessions are live and cohort-based, with recordings available afterward for review. This isn't a pre-recorded course.
Will I get a certificate?＋
Yes, on completing the final project and mentor critique you receive a certificate of completion from Architects Stories.
Can I join with only Foundation-level goals? ＋
Yes, you can enroll for Foundation alone, or the full three-level path. Each level is assessed separately.
